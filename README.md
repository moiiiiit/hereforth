# Hereforth

Hereforth is a proposed personal assistant for macOS. You can ask it a question, give it a task, or pick up a conversation that started in another app. It keeps a memory on your Mac across ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, and Granola.

Imagine asking, “What did we decide about the launch yesterday? Pick up the draft where I left off.” Hereforth should find the decision in your meeting notes, show you the source, and open a task with the draft and its earlier discussion. Later, you can ask how the work is going from your phone or let a teammate ask for the part you chose to share.

The project is in design and feasibility research. The intended release is open source. The first milestone is a native Mac app that can answer a question using evidence from two conversations and take you to both passages.

One team goal is to retire routine status syncs. Before a meeting, a teammate should be able to see what changed, what is blocked, and whose decision would move the work forward. Hereforth will let owners choose when their Mac is available to answer and track the model usage behind those updates.
